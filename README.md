Chipin is a unified group wallet that brings shared financial activity into a single system. Instead of using separate tools for splitting expenses, saving, bartering, and tracking environmental impact, Chipin records cash, green (carbon) credits, and time credits in one common ledger. Members can split and settle expenses with the help of smart settlement suggestions and a debt graph, save collectively through micro-savings round-ups, and exchange skills through a barter system protected by escrow and dispute resolution. A collaborative time capsule lets groups set aside goals and messages for a future date, while a trust score reflects each member’s reliability in settlements and trades. 
Imagine you and your friends share a lot of stuff: food, trips, favors. Normally you’d need a bunch of different apps to keep track of it all. Chipin puts everything in one shared wallet instead.
In that wallet, there are three kinds of “money”: real cash, green points for doing eco-friendly things, and time credits for the hours you spend helping each other.
Here’s what you can do with it (not limited to) 
●	Split bills: If someone pays for dinner, Chipin figures out who owes what, and it suggests the easiest way to pay everyone back. A chart shows who owes whom.
●	Save without thinking: Every payment gets rounded up, and the extra change goes into a shared savings pot.
●	Trade skills: If you help a friend with their homework, they pay you in time credits. The credits are held safely until the job is done, and if there’s an argument, there’s a system to sort it out.
●	Time capsule: The group saves goals and messages to open on a future date.
●	Trust score: The more reliable you are at paying people back and finishing what you promised, the higher your score.
